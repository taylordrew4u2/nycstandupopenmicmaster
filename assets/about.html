About the NYC open mic map

Find stand up comedy open mics across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island. Search by day, time, or venue.

Why I built it

I wanted one simple place to find a mic: a map, useful details, and no account needed to browse.

How listings work

Listings come from public directories and approved hosts. A claimed badge means a host can edit that mic; it is not a guarantee. Every listing shows its last update. Confirm details with the venue before going.

Hosts can add their names and social links. Only approved hosts get editing accounts.

Visitor counts use a random first-party browser cookie. No names, email addresses, or IP addresses are stored for analytics.

Who runs NYC Open Mic Master List?

NYC Open Mic Master List was created by Taylor Drew, a New York City stand-up comedian and producer of Pins & Needles Comedy.

Do I need an account to find an open mic?

No. Anyone can browse the map and listings. Host accounts are for approved hosts to update their own mics. Submitting a mic does not automatically claim it.

What do the listing badges mean?

Host claimed means an approved host can update the listing. Venue confirmed means the mic appears on an official venue website. Weekly mics repeat each week; biweekly mics run every other week. Pop-up mics are one-time events. These labels do not guarantee a mic will run; check the listing's sources and latest update before traveling.
